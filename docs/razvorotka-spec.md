# Razvorotka search

The English *Pattern Documentation Razvorotka* is the rule reference; the Russian copy describes the same pattern. Eleven annotated JPGs are visual references: six valid short, two valid long, and three invalid short. They lack instrument and candle timestamps, so exact OHLC matching is unavailable. The later *razvorotka_AI_learning_1.docx* reviews ten timestamped 1H cases and provides actionable corrections.

## Recognition contract

Razvorotka continues the preceding trend. Short requires a downtrend, consolidation, false breakout above the source, and return through at least 90% of its range; long is mirrored. The marker appears on the first closed Phase 3 candle that reaches the cancellation threshold. The documented weaker variant also qualifies when every source candle body is covered and exactly one source wick remains outside; its raw overlap is shown and labelled explicitly. Entry is optional and does not determine whether Phases 1–3 exist.

The source has at least 12 closed candles and repeated touches of both sides. A broad accumulation of at least 48 candles may have one side transition if both sides have at least three touches. Phase 2 needs a breakout extreme at least 15% of source height beyond the boundary and two consecutive complete candle bodies beyond the original boundary. A single outlier wick may be excluded from the displayed price boundary of a broad source, while the original boundary remains the confirmation threshold.

Search evaluates source windows of 12–96 bars. Phase 2 may include shallow returns or a crossing candle whose wick still overlaps the breakout zone. A close at least 20% back inside the source ends a confirmed false breakout even if that candle's wick crosses the boundary. A later breakout before a fully inside cancellation candle invalidates that attempt; an earlier return before two confirming bodies leaves no completed Phase 2 and a later attempt is scanned afresh. Phase 3 begins with a complete candle inside the source boundary. It cannot revisit the breakout side of that boundary, even with a wick, and must cover 90% of the raw source range within no more than the source duration, unless the documented single uncovered wick exception applies. The 20% transition threshold and source duration limit are implementation choices to separate contiguous phases; the latter is reinforced by the customer correction. A non-overlapping session opening gap against the false breakout invalidates that attempt. Matches sharing an unfinished formation collapse to the first Phase 3 marker.

Local, longer (up to 160 bars), and higher timeframe trends are checked. Clearly opposing or ranging context is rejected. A candidate is also rejected when both complete 64-bar and 160-bar windows before the actual source moved against its proposed direction; this permits a shorter pullback. When either window is unavailable, the result stays a candidate and the short prehistory is stated explicitly. A source nested inside a larger sideways range is rejected when the purported breakout only reaches its far edge; a nearby 24-bar check additionally requires three touches on separate days before calling that outer level established. A false breakout penetrating more than half of a repeatedly tested previous level without clearing that zone by its full height is rejected. These market structure checks are approximations of the customer's marked boundaries.

A feasible 1:2 risk/reward at the source edge or midpoint is required for publication as a candidate. Stop is just beyond the false breakout and target is the opposite source boundary. This is a hypothetical estimate, not an executed trade. Volume does not exist in the saved datasets, so the 6–11 candle exception and volume factors are not evaluated. Symmetry of earlier consolidations remains flagged for manual review.

The 1H scanner uses completed prepared 4H candles for higher context; the 4H scanner uses completed daily bars derived from prepared 1H history. Search respects known data breaks and uses no candle after its Phase 3 marker. Later candles are shown by default for visual review, but remain excluded from recognition. They can be hidden and replayed independently.

Examples 2 (BTC short), 5 (ETH short, corrected phase segmentation), and 10 (GLD short, extended source) are accepted at the marked candle. Examples 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, and 9 are rejected near the reviewed timestamp. The tests assert specific phase endpoints and verify that appending future BTC candles does not change the earlier signal. ## Reviewing the sample

The page combines all nine saved instruments and both timeframes, ordered by Phase 3 completion. The corrected rules expose 115 candidates in the current snapshot (90 on 1H, 25 on 4H), including two weakened single-wick cases. Filters narrow the same results without changing recognition thresholds. Every result is labelled a candidate because some context is uncertain and later price movement is not a completed trade.

The detector retains at least 160 available candles before the source, or three times the source length when greater. The selected chart expands this to the start of the current continuous data segment and shows every later candle through its end by default. A known data break or the start of history may shorten this view; the page displays the actual counts. A focus toggle magnifies the formation and nearest continuation. A user can hide the continuation, show only the nearest 80 hourly or 40 four-hour candles, and replay later candles one at a time or together. The existing pattern-review page likewise shows the full continuous segment around its selected signal without changing its detection rules.

All 120 previous plotted candidates and ten newly added cases were visually reviewed as an overview against the supplied examples; three previous cases were removed after measuring overlap against the raw range and four further cases after checking a contradictory preceding trend. Subsequent candle-level review removed six non-contiguous false breakout/cancellation attempts, one opening-gap case, and one NVDA 4H case nested in an established wider range. The review still found ambiguous wider-range and trend-context cases among the unlabelled results. They remain labelled as candidates, with explicit context uncertainty, because the customer's ten timestamped judgments do not establish ground truth for every historical event. The three accepted customer examples retain their corrected phase boundaries.

The scanner is a deterministic research tool over the available historical snapshots. Ten annotated examples are checked at the candle level; accuracy across unlabelled market history cannot be inferred from those alone.
